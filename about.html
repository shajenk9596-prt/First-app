How it started

Sports have always been how I keep my boys busy and active. Every season we signed up for something new: another team, another program, another nonprofit league. I loved it.

But the more we signed up for, the harder it got to actually get there. I’m a young mom doing this on a tight budget, and there were times I was running low on gas, or didn’t have the money or the support to get my boys to every practice and game.

Signing your kid up is the easy part. Getting them there is where it gets real.
I wasn’t the only one

Once I started paying attention, I realized other parents were dealing with the same thing: juggling work, other kids, and a gas gauge. And coaches and nonprofit organizations were out there working hard to give kids a place to play.

I thought, what if getting there didn’t have to be the hard part?
So I did something about it

FairPlay Rides gives young athletes safe, affordable rides to practices, games, and tournaments across Memphis. Parents book the rides they need. Coaches and nonprofits can set up rides for their whole team.

The goal is simple: no kid misses out because of a ride.
A little more about me

Before FairPlay Rides, I spent years in customer service, including handling escalated calls as a consumer advocate. That job taught me to listen well and stay calm when plans change.

Treat people the way I’d want to be treated.

That’s how I run FairPlay Rides, too.
