About FairPlay Rides

A mom’s answer to the ride problem every sports family knows.

SJ

Hi, I’m Shakenya

I’m a mom of three boys, ages 10, 8, and 6. Sports have always kept my sons busy and active. But the more teams and nonprofit programs we signed up for, the harder it got to get everyone where they needed to be. Running out of gas, or not having the money to get my boys to every practice and game, became a real struggle. And I quickly learned I wasn’t the only parent dealing with it.

So I started FairPlay Rides: safe, affordable rides for young athletes, so no kid misses a practice or game because of a ride. I also work with coaches and nonprofit organizations who want to get their whole team there.

After years in customer service, including handling escalated calls as a consumer advocate, I know how to communicate clearly and stay calm when plans change.

My promise: you’ll always know who’s driving, when they’re coming, and when your athlete arrives.

What FairPlay means

Fair prices

Flat fares by distance. The price we quote when you book is the price you pay.

Safe rides

Driver checks, parent permission forms, and a backup plan so no team gets stranded.

Every kid in the game

Cost shouldn’t decide who gets to play. Coaches and sponsors can prepay rides for their families.
