Quick answers

What if you’re late, or practice runs over?

We’ll keep you updated. Waiting time is free for the first 15 minutes, then $6 per 15 minutes.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel free up to 2 hours before pickup. After that, it’s $5.

Can siblings or a whole team ride together?

Yes. Siblings are a small add-on, and teams of 3 or more get a lower per-athlete rate.

What ages do you drive?

Athletes of all ages. Younger riders can use booster or car seats, and every athlete needs a parent permission form.
